Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 19, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your email address for account creation and the financial transaction details you log (amount, category, date, and descriptions). We do not collect sensitive banking credentials or credit card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the personal finance tracking services of Spendly. This includes displaying your spending summaries, generating reports, and managing your user account. We do not use your data for marketing or profiling.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure. You can delete your data by contacting us or through account settings (when available).

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable or financial information to outside parties. We may use trusted third-party hosting providers to store our data, who are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the handling of your data, please contact us at privacy@spendly.app.
